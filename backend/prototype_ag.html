Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Welcome to CIVICFORCE (Tamil Nadu Societal Problem & Resolution Engine, SIH-26043). By accessing our platform, reporting municipal issues, or participating as an administrative partner, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms.

1. Data Privacy & Collection

We collect geolocation data, reported imagery, and description details solely for the purpose of municipal triage and university R&D resolution. Personal contact details are handled securely under Government of Tamil Nadu data protocols.

2. Acceptable Use

Users must submit accurate and authentic infrastructure reports. Malicious or fraudulent reporting is subject to administrative review and deactivation.

3. Resolution SLA & Disclaimer

CIVICFORCE facilitates bridge connections between citizens and partner institutions. Resolution timelines depend on municipal departmental prioritization.
